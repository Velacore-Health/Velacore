What the women's HRT program is

A program for menopause and perimenopause. The options are an estradiol pill, patch or gel; estradiol vaginal cream, plus progesterone if needed; or a non-hormonal option, a paroxetine pill or a desvenlafaxine pill. Your clinician chooses the medication and the dose from your intake.

Velacore coordinates the visit, an independent licensed clinician decides whether to prescribe, and a licensed pharmacy fills the prescription. Whether any of these medications is appropriate for you is the clinician's decision.

Who this program is for

The clinician decides who is eligible from your intake and makes the final decision on every intake.

Every program is prescription only. Velacore coordinates access and education; the licensed clinician decides; a licensed pharmacy fills.

Women's HRT questions, answered

Which medications are offered?

Estradiol as a pill, patch or gel; estradiol vaginal cream, plus progesterone if needed; or a non-hormonal paroxetine pill or desvenlafaxine pill. Your clinician decides which, if any, is appropriate.

How am I billed?

$79 a month, charged as $237 every 12 weeks. Labs are not included.

Do I need labs?

Your clinician orders labs when they are merited. Labs are not in the program price and are paid by the patient.
