What the sexual health program is

The program covers prescription medications made from sildenafil, tadalafil, apomorphine, silodosin and L-citrulline, alone or combined, in three forms: single-dose vials, dissolving tablets, and a daily tadalafil pack. Your clinician chooses the medication, the form and the dose from your intake.

Velacore coordinates the visit, an independent licensed clinician decides whether to prescribe, and a licensed pharmacy fills the prescription. Whether any of these medications is appropriate for you is the clinician's decision.

Who this program is for

Adults 21 and over. Patients 75 and over are eligible only for single-ingredient medications. The clinician makes the final decision on every intake.

Every program is prescription only. Velacore coordinates access and education; the licensed clinician decides; a licensed pharmacy fills.

Sexual Health questions, answered

Which medications are offered?

Single-dose vials, 10 pack: sildenafil and tadalafil, $99; silodosin and tadalafil, $99; sildenafil, tadalafil and apomorphine, $109; sildenafil, tadalafil, apomorphine and L-citrulline, $119. Who is eligible?

Adults 21 and over. Patients 75 and over are eligible only for single-ingredient medications, which on this page means tadalafil daily. The clinician makes the final decision on every intake.

Do I need labs?

Your clinician will tell you if labs are recommended for you.
