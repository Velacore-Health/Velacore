How it works

Four steps from visit to doorstep

Velacore coordinates each step. An independent, licensed physician makes every prescribing decision.

  1. STEP 01

    Start your visit

    Complete a secure online intake about your goals, history, and medications. Free to begin.

  2. STEP 02

    Physician review

    An independent, licensed physician reviews it and decides whether to prescribe — on clinical judgment alone.

  3. STEP 03

    Pharmacy fulfillment

    If prescribed, a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy fills your treatment and ships it to you.

  4. STEP 04

    Ongoing support

    Your care team helps you coordinate follow-ups and reorders throughout your treatment.

  5. GUARANTEE

    Refund if not prescribed

    If the physician doesn't prescribe, you're refunded in full — including the review fee — within 15 days.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Velacore Health?

Velacore Health is a telehealth platform. We coordinate access to independent, licensed physicians and 503A compounding pharmacies. We do not provide, guarantee, or facilitate any medical services, prescriptions, or treatments — all prescribing decisions are made solely by the independent physician.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Every treatment is prescription only. You complete an online visit, and an independent, licensed physician decides whether to prescribe based solely on clinical judgment. Prescriptions are issued only when clinically appropriate.

Does Velacore prescribe or provide medical care?

No. Velacore coordinates access and logistics. Independent, licensed physicians make all clinical decisions, and licensed 503A pharmacies handle fulfillment.

What is your refund policy?

If the independent physician does not prescribe, you're refunded in full — including your program payment and the physician review fee — within 15 days. Medications are non-returnable once dispensed.

Which states do you serve?

Velacore Health telehealth services are currently available to residents of select US states. Contact our care team to confirm availability in your state.

How do I get started?

Start an online visit. It's free to begin and helps the independent physician determine whether treatment is appropriate for you — with no obligation.
