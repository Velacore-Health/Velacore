Dourado asked Vale to ask Kevin what his uploaded videos teach about buttons, tiles, gradients and marketing. Kevin answered with general knowledge only, no files from other projects. Shown means the creator showed it in the video; inferred means it is Kevin's own addition. Nothing here is a conversion number: none of the videos gave one.
