## What it confirms

- Their prices match ours to the dollar: Semaglutide injection $209, Tirzepatide $249, oral Semaglutide $259, Microdosing $179, NAD+ $229, Sermorelin $229, Glutathione injection $145, every 4 weeks. This backs the Nov 20 price change and the $259 oral price.
- Oral semaglutide on Agile is a tablet that dissolves under the tongue, taken in the morning on an empty stomach.
- The eight live video visit states are the same as our list.
- They do not name OpenLoop anywhere. Their clinical standards page says their partnership agreement keeps the clinical partner unnamed in patient materials. Our site does not name OpenLoop either. Expect the same clause in our MSA.
- They use three different OpenLoop intake links: weight loss, microdosing, and longevity for NAD+, Sermorelin and Glutathione. Johnny wrote one link covers everything; ask for per-program links when ours arrive.
- Google Analytics runs on their public pages; no Meta pixel was found in the page source.

## What their site says that answers our open questions

Bold You's own words, not OpenLoop's in writing, so unconfirmed until OpenLoop answers:
- The card is charged at checkout; monthly plans renew every 28 days, 13 charges a year, and they show the first year total.
- Full refund and a written reason if the provider declines.
- Pause for up to 60 days.
- Most patients hear from a clinician in 1 to 2 days; the intake takes about 8 minutes.
- A short refill check-in about 5 days before each refill.
- Injectables ship cold packed, in plain packaging.
- Labs: on GLP-1 weight loss, labs if ordered are paid by the partner; on microdosing, labs are recommended but not required, at no extra cost; on HRT, labs if ordered are billed by the lab.
- Microdosing is sold as injection only, semaglutide or tirzepatide, with the provider choosing the formulation. Our site offers an oral microdosing option, from the Nov 2 proposal.
- Longevity programs come in monthly, 3 month and 6 month plans. Our pricing page says 24 and 52 week plans are offered for every program.
- They publish a care team phone number.
- Their Start page collects name, email, phone, state and the program of interest, with a text message opt-in, before sending the patient to the OpenLoop intake.
