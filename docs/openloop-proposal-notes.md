# OpenLoop proposal, what the site needs to know

Source: "Velacore Health - OpenLoop - Proposal - 11_02_26.pdf", 20 pages, dated Nov 2 2026, expires about Dec 2 2026. The PDF itself stays out of the repo. This file holds only the patient-facing facts and the terms that shape the site. The column showing what Velacore receives per program is deliberately left out; it is a cost sheet and lives only in the PDF.

Every price below is marked by OpenLoop as an example, subject to change, and availability can change at any time with no notice. Nothing here goes on the site as a quote until the agreement is signed.

## Who does what

- OpenLoop: patient billing and the merchant account, clinical consults, ordering and paying for base weight-loss labs at Quest or Labcorp, pharmacy fulfillment and medication cost, 24/7/365 inbound patient support, supply chain across all 50 states plus DC.
- Velacore: patient education, non-clinical patient support, the technology platform, meaning the website.
- Providers: MD, DO and NP, licensed in all 50 states, available 7am to 7pm CST, no holiday coverage. Malpractice coverage included.
- States that require a live video visit for the first prescription: AR, DC, DE, KS, MS, NM, RI, WV. Everywhere else the first review is async, with video available on request.
- Clinician credentialing, Schedule B of the proposal: primary source background check, education and training review, work history, board certification review, state board and licensing review, DEA review and licensing, NPI verification, liability coverage, malpractice history, Medicaid sanctions review, NPDB review, OIG sanction and exclusion review, signed attestations. These are the facts behind the "credentialed before care" copy on the site.
- The microdosing program, Schedule D: unlimited clinician consults, async on a 12 week cycle with video any time, and 24/7/365 live support "with full staff of CMAs and RNs for escalations". The RN line is stated for microdosing only, so the site does not promise nurses for every program.

## Terms that rewrite the legal pages

- Cancel any time for any reason. Refund for any reason at any time, except a month whose medication has already been ordered. No separate physician fee of any kind.
- Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, FSA and HSA cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay. No American Express.
- Patients are charged each period per their subscription terms. Medication on 12, 24 and 52 week plans ships every four weeks.
- GLP-1 eligibility: age 18 to 74 to start, BMI 27 or above, or 25 or above with a related condition. Microdosing: BMI over 20, age 18 to 74. Sexual health: 21 and over.
- Labs: base weight-loss labs are paid by OpenLoop when done at Quest or Labcorp. Labs for HRT are paid by the patient. Labs can be done after starting medication when nothing in intake says they are urgent.
- OpenLoop's own note: all patient-facing materials must carry the legal and regulatory disclosures, including about compounded medications.
- Optional care coaching exists at $10 per patient per month. Velacore keeps it off. No coaching language on the site.

## Unresolved, must be settled in the MSA before launch

- The proposal describes patient intake and credit-card entry happening "through Velacore Health's interface" with an API into OpenLoop's EHR. The Nov 4 demo described Launchpad as OpenLoop-hosted, Velacore-branded intake and portal pages that we only link to. The site plan assumes the demo version, links out, no card entry on our site. Get the Launchpad hosted model in writing.
- Retail price is ours to set within a floor and ceiling per the demo. The ceiling is not in the proposal. Our planned $229 and $279 month-to-month prices sit above the proposal's $209 and $249 examples, so confirm they are allowed.
- Glutathione is not in the proposal at all. It appeared in the demo. Confirm or drop it.
- Tirzepatide oral appears in the dose table but has no price page.

## Patient journey, for the How it works section

1. Intake online. Reviewed async by a licensed clinician, or by scheduled video where the state requires it.
2. If appropriate, medication is prescribed and labs ordered. Base labs are paid by OpenLoop.
3. Day 30: async review of labs and intake, refill if eligible.
4. Monthly async review until the dose is steady, then quarterly, with video visits available on request.
5. For GLP-1s the clinician may also prescribe an optional nausea medication to pick up at a local pharmacy.
6. NAD+ and sermorelin: 4 week first prescription, then 12 week refills. B12 MIC: 12 week prescriptions.
