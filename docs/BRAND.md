# Velacore Health brand bible

Started Nov 6 2026. This is the one place for approved logos, colors, type, voice and image rules. Anything made for Velacore, on the site, in an ad, in a Gemini prompt, on a label or in a deck, follows this file. When something here changes, change it here first.

## 0. Who owns the brand

Velacore Health is a brand of First Venture Group LLC, the parent company. The public-facing name is always Velacore Health. First Venture Group LLC appears only where the legal entity must: the footer copyright line, the Terms of Service, contracts, invoices and the business email address. It never appears on a logo, a label, an ad or a product image.

## 1. The mark

A double helix with two leaves, set inside a thin ring. It says biology plus growth.

The designer's package, downloaded Nov 6 2026 from the business Drive into `brand/logo/`, holds three versions of the full logo, each as .ai, .png and .jpg at 1507 by 1135, version 01 also as .eps, .pdf and .svg:

- `VelaCore Health_01`: the mark in the gradient, the wordmark in white. For dark grounds. The .svg and .ai are the vector source for everything else.
- `VelaCore Health_02`: mark and wordmark all white. For dark grounds and one-color print.
- `VelaCore Health_03`: mark and wordmark all black. For light grounds and one-color print.

The wordmark is outlined paths in the source, so the typeface name is not recorded anywhere in the package. Treat the lettering as artwork; never reset it in a web font.

Site files, derived from the package before it was found:

- `public/logo-full.png`, 317 by 231: the gradient mark with the wordmark in black on white. Not one of the three designer versions as delivered; it is a light-ground edit. Re-export it from the .ai at 2x once a light-ground version is agreed, see open item 2.
- `public/logo-mark.png`, 119 by 128: the mark alone, recolored to jade. A derived asset, not from the designer; it is the header and favicon mark on the site and the mark on the product label. Keep it, but know it is ours, not the designer's.

Rules:
- Never redraw, recolor or stretch the mark. Never add a drop shadow or an outline.
- On white or mint, use either version. On pine or any dark ground, use the jade mark with white wordmark text, never the gradient logo.
- Clear space around the logo is at least the height of the letter V in VELACORE.
- Minimum size on screen: 28 pixels tall for the mark, 110 pixels wide for the full logo.
- The label on product vials uses the mark above the wordmark, both in pine, no gradient. See section 6.

Source: `brand/logo/VelaCore Health_01/VelaCore Health-01.svg` and `VelaCore Health.ai`. Print and large-format uses are cleared.

## 3. Type

- Headlines: Fraunces, weight 500, optical size high, the SOFT axis around 30 to 50. Sentence case. No single word colored or italic inside a headline.
- Body, buttons, labels, navigation: Hanken Grotesk, 400 to 600.
- Logo wordmark: the logo's own lettering. It is not a web font we have. Do not try to set VELACORE in Fraunces or Hanken; use the logo file.
- Sizes on the site: body 17 px, line length under 80 characters, headlines from 2.6 rem to 4.4 rem.
- Nothing in all caps except the logo wordmark and the product label. No tracked-out small-caps labels.

Both web fonts load from Google Fonts. Desktop files for Canva or print: download Fraunces and Hanken Grotesk from Google Fonts, both are free under the Open Font License.

## 4. Voice

Plain, calm, specific. Velacore coordinates; the licensed clinician decides; the 503A pharmacy fills, always in that order. No outcome promises, no weight numbers, no brand-name drug comparisons, no coaching language, no "FDA approved" near a compounded medication. Sentence case. No em dashes, no parentheses, no emojis, no exclamation marks. Full rules live in `COMPLIANCE.md` and in the `vc-copy` agent.

## 5. Images

Changed Nov 11 2026 at Dourado's direction: the site must read as health and wellness, so it carries more people and the vials sit in clean water.

Product shots: the vial upright in a shallow sheet of perfectly clear water on white, a crisp splash around its base, a few droplets in the air, soft mint background fading to white, cool daylight from the upper left. The label stays dry, sharp and exactly as approved. The Style A label from section 6, a deep matte pine cap with a silver crimp on every vial, clear glass. The label wraps the curved glass edge to edge, seen from slightly above, so its edges and text curve with the vial. No people in product shots, no props, no marble, no sparkles, no glow, no colored water.

Lifestyle photos: candid everyday routines in real homes and neighborhoods, cool natural daylight, white and pale mint surroundings with one touch of green. Adults about 35 to 60, leaning to the late thirties and forties so the site never reads as a senior brand, by Dourado's direction Nov 11 2026; no gray-haired couples as the lead image. Varied backgrounds, ordinary builds, current casual clothes. A well-behaved dog on a walk is welcome. Cooking, walking, coffee, a laptop at a kitchen table, a video call from the sofa, a parcel on a doorstep. Several per page are fine where each one illustrates a step or a section. Never: gyms or workout gear, model-thin or fitness-model bodies, scales, tape measures, mirrors, waistlines, before and after, pills, needles or injections, and no name, caption or quote that presents a person as a Velacore patient.

Every image passes the reject list in `docs/gemini-prompts.md` before it goes on the site.

Illustrations: thin pine or jade line icons on mint, as on the education cards. No 3D renders, no clip art, no stock-looking isometric scenes.

Files: product images are `public/images/products/<program>.webp` and people and scenes are `public/images/lifestyle/<name>.webp`, one fixed name each. Drop Gemini output in `assets-in/products/` or `assets-in/lifestyle/` and run `tools/prepare_images.py`. Prompts are in `docs/gemini-prompts.md`.

## 6. The product label

Every vial, bottle and spray carries the same label, so the shelf reads as one brand:

Style A, pine band, chosen by Dourado Nov 11 2026:

Top panel, pine, the upper 44 percent: the designer's full logo, mark above the VELACORE HEALTH wordmark, reversed out in white as in version 02
A fine line in the program color under the panel
The program name in Fraunces, pine, title case, as large as fits
A short dash in the program color
Small, pine, wide spaced capitals: FOR PRESCRIPTION USE ONLY / PRESCRIPTION COMPOUNDED / 503A COMPOUNDING PHARMACY
Rx ONLY in a thin pine outlined pill

The approved artwork is `brand/label/label-<program>.png`, made by `brand/label/make_labels.py`. Image prompts attach it so the label text comes out exact. Never let an image tool write its own label: on Nov 11 2026 Gemini replaced a label with research-use wording, which is never acceptable on a Velacore vial.

Nothing else. No seals, no stars, no "FDA", no benefit words, no dosage claims on the mock label.

Every vial cap is deep matte pine, #15302A, with a silver crimp. The program color appears only in the label's thin line and dash. Amber glass for the microdosing dropper, white HDPE for any pill bottle, white for nasal sprays.
