### h2: If this is an emergency

Telehealth is not for emergencies. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Do not use this website, email or the patient portal for urgent help.

### h2: Who does what

Velacore Health coordinates. We run this website, explain how the programs work and answer non-medical questions.

An independent licensed clinician decides. The clinicians are physicians and nurse practitioners licensed in your state and engaged through our telehealth services partner. They review your intake and decide, on their own clinical judgment, whether to prescribe. Velacore does not practice medicine, does not employ the clinicians and has no say in that decision.

A licensed 503A compounding pharmacy fills. If a clinician prescribes, the pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to you.

Using this website does not create a clinician and patient relationship with Velacore.

### h2: Who your clinician is

Clinicians are credentialed before they review intakes. That includes checks of education and training, state licenses, board certification, work history and malpractice coverage.

You have the right to know who is treating you. You can ask for your clinician's name, license type and license number, and you can check any license with the licensing board in your state.

### h2: What telehealth is

Telehealth means a licensed clinician reviews your health information and cares for you remotely, without an in-person visit.

Most reviews are asynchronous. You fill in the intake, the clinician reads it and responds, and there is no live appointment. You can ask for a video visit at any time at no extra cost. A video visit is required for the first prescription in Arkansas, the District of Columbia, Delaware, Kansas, Mississippi, New Mexico, Rhode Island and West Virginia.

Clinicians are available 7am to 7pm Central Time. Patient support is available 24 hours a day, every day.

### h2: Possible benefits

- You can reach a licensed clinician from home.
- You do not need to travel or wait in an office.
- If you are prescribed, the pharmacy ships to your door.

### h2: Risks and limits

- The clinician does not examine you in person. They rely on what you tell them. Information that is incomplete or wrong can affect your care.
- A prescription is never guaranteed. It is issued only when the clinician finds it clinically appropriate.
- Technology can fail. A connection can drop, a message can be delayed, a video can freeze. If that happens during a visit, the visit may need to be rescheduled.
- The clinician may decide telehealth is not right for your situation and refer you to in-person care.
- No electronic system is perfectly secure, even with safeguards in place.
- Every medication carries possible risks and side effects. The clinician and the pharmacy give you the information that comes with your prescription.

### h2: In-person care is always an option

You can choose in-person care instead of telehealth, or alongside it, at any time. Telehealth through this platform does not replace a primary care clinician. Keep your regular doctor informed about any medication you are prescribed.

### h2: What these programs cover

The programs on this site are Tirzepatide, Semaglutide, Microdosing GLP-1, NAD+, Glutathione and Sermorelin. Every one is prescription only. Prescription required.

Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

### h2: Labs

For the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs, the clinician may order baseline labs through a national laboratory at no cost to you. In most cases you can start medication before the results are back. For other programs, the clinician will tell you if labs are recommended.

Labs ordered through a program are limited to what the clinician needs for that program. They are not a full health screening and do not replace the tests your regular doctor may order. Velacore does not order, receive or read lab results.

### h2: Privacy and its limits

Your intake answers and health records are held by our telehealth services partner, the clinicians and the pharmacy. They are not stored on this website. Those parties are responsible for keeping your health information confidential under federal and state law.

Confidentiality has legal limits. Health care providers can be required to share information in certain situations, such as a court order, a public health reporting duty, or a serious and immediate risk to someone's safety. The Notice of Privacy Practices you receive at intake explains these in full. See the Health information and HIPAA page, draft `02-health-information-and-hipaa.md`.

### h2: Fees

Programs are paid out of pocket. They are not billed to insurance, and neither Velacore nor our partner files insurance claims for you. FSA and HSA cards are accepted. The program price is shown before you start. There is no separate consultation or physician fee.

Our telehealth services partner charges your payment method and appears on your card statement. If you think a charge is wrong, contact support first. See the Refund Policy, draft `06-refund-policy-additions.md`.

### h2: Who can use the service

You must be 18 or older and located in the United States. GLP-1 programs are for adults 18 to 74 and have body mass index criteria, which the clinician applies. The clinician makes the final decision on every intake.

### h2: What you will be asked to agree to

When you start a visit on our partner's intake, you will be asked to confirm points like these. This list is a summary, not the consent itself.

- You understand that care is delivered by telehealth and what that means.
- The information you give is true and complete, and you will update it if your health changes.
- You are 18 or older and you are the person the intake describes.
- You are physically located in the state you named at the time of your visit.
- Your health information may be sent electronically, and may cross state lines, between the clinician, the partner and the pharmacy so your care can be delivered.
- Technology can fail, and a visit may be interrupted or rescheduled.
- A prescription is not guaranteed.
- If someone else is in the room during a video visit, that is your choice, and the clinician may ask who is present.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing ends telehealth care going forward. It does not undo care already given, and it does not affect a month whose medication has already been ordered.
- You will seek in-person or emergency care when you need it.
