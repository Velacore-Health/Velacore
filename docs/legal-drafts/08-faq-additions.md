## Part A. Answers the sources support

### When am I charged?

Our telehealth services partner charges your payment method, and its name is the one on your card statement. Month to month programs are charged each billing period, and prepaid plans are charged up front and shipped every four weeks. If the clinician does not prescribe, your program payment is refunded in full.

Source: proposal notes, "charged each period per their subscription terms" and the plan shipping line. Live refund policy for the rest.

Left out on purpose: the exact moment of the first charge. See part B, question 1.

### How do I reorder or cancel?

You do not need to reorder. Your program continues each billing period, and the clinician reviews your intake updates before refills. You can cancel at any time for any reason from your patient portal or by contacting support, and a month whose medication has already been ordered will still ship.

Source: live FAQ and refund policy, proposal notes patient journey.

### How do I change my card or shipping address?

Contact patient support, which is available 24 hours a day, every day. Velacore does not hold your card or address, so we cannot change them for you.

Source: live home page, "your patient portal handles refills, changes and cancellation". The exact screens are a part B question.

### What is the minimum age?

You must be 18 or older and located in the United States. The Tirzepatide, Semaglutide and Microdosing GLP-1 programs are for adults 18 to 74. The clinician makes the final decision on every intake.

Source: proposal notes eligibility, live FAQ. This is an eligibility answer. Body mass index criteria are already in the live "Who is eligible?" answer and are not repeated.

### Is it legal to get a prescription online?

Every intake is reviewed by a clinician licensed in your state, and a few states require a live video visit before the first prescription. The clinician decides whether to prescribe, and nothing is guaranteed.

Source: proposal notes on licensing in all 50 states and the video-visit states.

## Part B. Needs OpenLoop, with the exact question to ask

No answer is drafted for these. The sources do not support one.

1. When am I charged, the first time?
   Ask: "At what moment is the patient's card first charged: when they submit the intake, or only after the clinician approves? If at intake, is it a charge or an authorization hold, and how fast is it reversed when the clinician declines?"

2. Why do you need a photo ID and a selfie?
   Ask: "Does the intake ask for a photo ID, a selfie or both? What is each used for, who sees them, where are they stored and for how long? Please send wording we may use to explain this to patients."

3. Do I need to sign for delivery?
   Ask: "Which carriers do the pharmacies use, is a signature required on delivery for any program or any state, and is cold packaging used?"

4. How do I change my card or shipping address, step by step?
   Ask: "Where in the portal does a patient change the card on file and the shipping address, and what is the cutoff before a shipment for an address change to apply?"

5. Can I use my local pharmacy?
   Ask: "Can a patient have a program prescription sent to a local pharmacy of their choice, or are program medications filled only by the partner 503A pharmacies? Please confirm in writing."
   Note for Vale: the proposal says the clinician may also prescribe an optional nausea medication for pickup at a local pharmacy on GLP-1 programs. That is not the same as filling the program medication locally, and it is not on the site today. Whether to mention it is a vc-reviewer call.

6. How long until delivery?
   Ask: "What is the typical time from intake to clinician decision, and from prescription to delivery? May we publish those as typical times, and in what words?"
   The live answer gives no number, which is right until this is in writing.

7. How do I pause a program?
   Ask: "Can a patient pause a subscription without cancelling, for how long, and where?"
   The live FAQ question reads "Can I cancel or pause any time?" but the answer covers cancelling only. Either get this answered or drop "or pause" from the question.

8. Is there an age limit for NAD+, Glutathione and Sermorelin?
   Ask: "What are the age and other eligibility criteria for the NAD+, Glutathione and Sermorelin programs?"
   The proposal gives ranges for GLP-1 and microdosing only.

9. Glutathione, everything.
   Ask: "Please confirm Glutathione in the written Agile proposal: the form, the price, the eligibility rules and the refill rhythm."
