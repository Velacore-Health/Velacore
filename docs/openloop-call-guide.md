# OpenLoop Call Guide — Velacore Health

**Call:** Nov 2, 2:45 PM CST · **Goal:** get facts we can compare to Asher, not to commit.
**After the call:** upload the recording transcript and I'll score it against this guide.

## 0. Before the call (15 minutes of homework)

1. **Read your Asher Sign-Up Agreement's termination, exclusivity and non-compete sections.** If you
   can't leave cleanly, nothing OpenLoop says matters yet.
2. **Pick three volume scenarios** so every price they quote can be compared: **25, 100 and 300
   patients/month.** Cash is tight, so the 25 case matters most.
3. **Know your current Asher numbers:** monthly membership (you said ~$350), active patient count,
   average retail price per program.
4. **Search the HHS breach portal (ocrportal.hhs.gov) for "OpenLoop".** One comparison blog reported a
   January 2026 breach. It's unverified. Check it yourself before the call.
5. **Tell them on the call that you're recording** and get a verbal OK. Some states require consent
   from everyone on the call.

## 1. Opening (60 seconds)

> "I run Velacore Health, a direct-to-consumer telehealth brand: GLP-1s, NAD+, glutathione and
> sermorelin. I'm on another partner platform today and I'm planning to scale through Meta and Google
> ads on a Shopify site. I'm comparing options and want to understand cost, control and compliance.
> I won't be deciding on this call. I'd like a written proposal after."

Don't share Asher's pricing or contract terms. Say "another platform" and stop there.

## 2. Must-ask questions (in priority order)

### A. Money — ask first, get numbers
1. What's the **all-in cost per patient per month** at 25, 100 and 300 patients? Include setup fee,
   monthly platform fee, per-consult/provider fee, any revenue share, and shipping.
2. Is there a **revenue share**? What percentage, of what (gross or net)?
3. **Minimum commitment**: contract length, monthly minimums, auto-renewal?
4. What does the **$250** figure my friend mentioned actually cover? What's NOT included?
5. **Medication cost to us** for each of our five products, at each strength/supply. Can we set our
   own retail price?

### B. Control — who owns what
6. **Who is merchant of record?** Do patients pay into OpenLoop's merchant account or ours? Who holds
   the card tokens? Can we use our own processor with our Shopify checkout?
7. **Who owns the patient list and billing relationship**, especially if we leave?
8. Is it truly **our brand**: our domain, our product pages, our label on the vial?
9. "We'd have to use all your peptides." **Is the formulary fixed?** Can we sell our five exactly,
   and which pharmacies fill them?

### C. LegitScript and advertising — this decides whether ads can run
10. How do you help us get **LegitScript certified in our own company's name**? Cost? Timeline?
    Who files and who pays? Does OpenLoop's own certification cover our site, or just theirs?
11. Which of our products can be **advertised on Meta and Google**, and how do your other brands
    handle that?
12. **Compounded GLP-1s:** the FDA sent many warning letters in 2026 and treats B12 or other add-ons as
    still "essentially a copy." How do you and your pharmacies handle that? Who carries that risk?
13. Do you provide **ad-compliant copy or review** of our site and ads?

### D. Technology — how automatic can it really be?
14. Walk me through the **patient journey from our Shopify checkout to the prescription shipping.**
    What's automated and what's manual?
15. Do you have an **API and webhooks**? Is there a Shopify integration? Who builds it and what does it
    cost?
16. Can patients **re-order and renew** without us touching it? How are refills triggered?
17. How do we **see order and prescription status**: dashboard, API, emails?

### E. Clinical and compliance
18. Are your providers licensed in **all 50 states** (specifically AK, MS, NJ)? Typical turnaround from
    intake to decision? Who is the **medical director**?
19. How does the **$20-style provider fee** work (do you use a CPOM/MSO structure), and what happens
    to fees when a patient is declined?
20. **HIPAA:** will you sign a **BAA** before we send any patient data? Do you have a SOC 2 report,
    and which type? **Have you had a reportable breach?** (Ask directly and calmly.)
21. Who handles **patient support and clinical questions**, and what are response times?

### F. Contract and moving over
22. **Exit terms:** what happens to our patients, data and prescriptions if we leave?
23. **Exclusivity or non-compete?** Can price or terms change mid-contract?
24. Can you **move our existing patients** and keep renewals continuous? Timeline for launch?
25. **References:** two or three direct-to-consumer brands of our size on peptides/GLP-1 that I can
    call, ideally ones that advertise.

## 3. Red flags — note them if you hear them

- Won't give any number until you "sign up" or "get a demo."
- Setup fee is large *and* there's a long term with a revenue share.
- Patients pay into **their** merchant account and you can't change that.
- "LegitScript is handled" without saying whose name it's in.
- Can't answer the compounded-GLP-1 FDA question, or says it's "not a concern."
- Won't commit to a **BAA** or give a straight breach answer.
- Pressure to decide this week.

## 4. Closing (do all four)

1. "Please send a **written proposal** with all-in pricing at 25/100/300 patients and the contract
   terms within two business days."
2. "Please send the **BAA** and the **master agreement** so my advisor can review them."
3. Ask for the **references** from question 25.
4. "I'll decide after comparing in writing. Don't sign anything on the call."

## 6. Decision rule

**Switch only if all of these are true:**
- OpenLoop's **all-in cost per patient is lower** than Asher's at your realistic volume,
  **including setup**.
- **You keep the brand and the patient relationship**, and can leave with your data.
- **LegitScript ends up in your name**, with a defined cost and timeline.
- You get a **BAA** and a straight answer on security.
- The setup fee and terms don't put your cash at risk before you earn from ads.

If even one fails, stay with Asher and use OpenLoop's numbers to negotiate or revisit later.
