  1. 1

    Answer a few questions online

    Pick a program and complete a secure intake about your goals, health history and medications.

    • Not sure which program? Find your program walks you through it

    More about this step

    You start by choosing a program. If you are not sure which one, Find your program walks you through the options. The clinician still makes the final decision on which program, if any, is appropriate.

    The intake is a set of questions about your goals, your health history and the medications you take. It runs on our telehealth services partner's secure pages, not on this website, so you will see their name there instead of ours.

    The Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs are for adults 18 to 74 with a body mass index of 27 or above, or 25 or above with a related health condition. The Microdosing GLP-1 program is for adults 18 to 74 with a body mass index over 20. Each program page states who the program is for.

  2. 2

    A licensed clinician reviews it

    A physician or nurse practitioner licensed in your state reviews your intake and decides whether to prescribe.

    • Most reviews happen without a live appointment
    • You can request a video visit through patient support
    • Velacore has no say in the decision
    More about this step

    A physician or nurse practitioner licensed in your state reviews your intake. The clinician decides whether to prescribe, based solely on clinical judgment. Velacore has no say in that decision.

    Most reviews happen without a live appointment. A video visit is required for the first prescription in Arkansas, the District of Columbia, Delaware, Kansas, Mississippi, New Mexico, Rhode Island and West Virginia. Everywhere else a video visit is optional, and you can ask patient support for one at any time.

    Clinician hours are 7am to 7pm Central Time, Monday to Friday, not including holidays. More about who the clinicians are and how they are credentialed is on the clinicians page.

  3. 3

    A pharmacy prepares and ships it

    If you are prescribed, a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to you.

    • Ongoing plans ship every four weeks
    • Baseline labs at a national laboratory, at no cost to you when the clinician orders them, on the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs

    Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

    More about this step

    If the clinician prescribes, a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy prepares your medication to that prescription and ships it to you. Ongoing plans ship every four weeks.

    Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

    For the Semaglutide, Tirzepatide and Microdosing GLP-1 programs, labs are not required before your first prescription. The clinician orders them only if they decide labs are needed. For the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs, baseline labs the clinician orders are done at a national laboratory at no cost to you. For other programs the clinician will tell you if labs are recommended.

    A 503A pharmacy prepares medication for one named patient from a valid prescription. Our article on what a 503A compounding pharmacy is explains the term.

  4. 4

    Reviews and support continue

    The clinician reviews your intake updates on a set schedule, and patient support is available 24 hours a day, every day.

    • Nothing about your prescription changes without a clinician's review
    • Message patient support, who bring in a clinician when needed
    More about this step

    A prescription is not a one-time event. The clinician reviews your intake updates on a schedule that depends on the program.

    • Semaglutide and Tirzepatide. Each month you update a short intake. The clinician reviews it with any lab results and decides whether to hold or change the dose. Once your dose is steady, reviews move to every three months.
    • Microdosing GLP-1. The first prescription is a 4-week supply. After that you update a short intake every 12 weeks and the clinician reviews it.
    • NAD+, Glutathione and Sermorelin. The first prescription is a 4-week supply. The clinician reviews an intake update at 3 to 4 weeks before writing a 12-week refill, and reviews continue every 12 weeks.

    Nothing about your prescription changes without a clinician's review.

    Patient support is available 24 hours a day, every day. You can message patient support or ask for a video visit through your patient portal. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Not prescribed? Your program payment is refunded in full.

A prescription is not guaranteed. After you start, you can cancel at any time, and you can request a refund for any billing period whose medication has not yet shipped. Medications are non-returnable once dispensed.

What Velacore does and does not do

Velacore does

  • We run this website.
  • We explain the programs and show their prices.
  • We point you to the secure intake.
  • We answer questions about how the service works.

Velacore does not

  • We do not practice medicine or give medical advice.
  • We do not prescribe, and we have no say in whether a clinician prescribes.
  • We do not make, compound or ship medication.
  • We do not hold your card details. Our telehealth services partner bills you.
  • We do not promise that you will be prescribed anything.

The intake, the clinician review, billing and patient support are run by our telehealth services partner on its own secure pages.

Common questions

Do I have to speak with a clinician live?

In most states, no. Most reviews happen without a live appointment. A video visit is required for the first prescription in Arkansas, the District of Columbia, Delaware, Kansas, Mississippi, New Mexico, Rhode Island and West Virginia.

Do I need labs before I start medication?

For the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs, no, not before your first prescription. The clinician orders labs only if they decide labs are needed. That decision belongs to the clinician.

How often does medication ship?

Ongoing plans ship every four weeks.

Does Velacore decide whether I am prescribed?

No. The independent licensed clinician decides, based solely on clinical judgment. Velacore coordinates access and education.
