Refund Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Refund Policy is part of, and incorporated into, our Terms of Service. By starting an online visit or purchasing a program, you agree to it.

Our physician-approval promise

Velacore Health is a telehealth platform that coordinates access to independent, licensed physicians and 503A compounding pharmacies. Our refund promise is tied to the physician approval process.

If the independent reviewing physician does not approve your program — in full or in part — your program payment is refunded in full within 15 days of that decision, to the original payment method.

Physician fee

A one-time $20 physician fee is collected when you complete your intake, on behalf of the independent medical group — not Velacore Health. It covers the independent physician's clinical review of your intake, which is performed regardless of whether a prescription is ultimately issued, and is therefore non-refundable. This fee is separate from the cost of any medication or program.

Medications

Medications are non-returnable and non-refundable once dispensed by the pharmacy, as required by federal and state pharmacy law. This applies even if the medication is unused or unopened.

Shipping

Shipping is included at no additional charge — there is no separate shipping fee.

Subscriptions & cancellations

Some programs are offered on a recurring basis. You may cancel at any time before your next fulfillment date; cancellation stops future charges but does not retroactively refund a fulfillment that has already been dispensed. To cancel, contact us at hello@velacorehealth.com.

How to request a refund

Email hello@velacorehealth.com with your name and order details. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 15 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@velacorehealth.com.
