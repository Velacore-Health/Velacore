What NAD+ is

NAD+ is a coenzyme the body makes and uses in cellular energy metabolism. In this program it is prepared by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy as an injection, a nasal spray or an oral dropper, at a dose your clinician sets.

Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy. Whether NAD+ is appropriate for you is the clinician's decision.

NAD+ questions, answered

Which form should I choose?

Your clinician decides the form and dose from your intake. The injection, nasal spray and oral dropper deliver different amounts per dose.

Do I need labs?

Not as a rule. Your clinician will tell you if labs are recommended for you.

What does the price include?

The medication, supplies, clinician reviews, shipping, and patient support around the clock. There is no separate consultation or physician fee.

What if I am not prescribed?

Nothing is ordered and your program payment is refunded in full.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time for any reason from your patient portal. Any month whose medication has not been ordered is refunded.
