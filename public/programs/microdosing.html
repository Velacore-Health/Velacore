What the microdosing program is

The program uses a GLP-1 medication at a lower dose than the standard weight programs, combined in one preparation with ingredients such as NAD+, B12, L-carnitine, inositol, methionine, choline, magnesium or L-theanine. There are seven formulations, in oral or injectable form, and your clinician picks the one that fits your intake.

Each preparation is compounded by a licensed 503A pharmacy to your clinician's prescription. Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy. Ingredient availability can vary by state and pharmacy.

Who this program is for

Adults 18 to 74 with a body mass index over 20. The clinician makes the final decision on every intake.

Every program is prescription only. Velacore coordinates access and education; the licensed clinician decides; the 503A pharmacy fills.

Microdosing GLP-1 questions, answered

How is this different from the standard semaglutide or tirzepatide programs?

The GLP-1 dose is lower, it is combined with other ingredients in one preparation, there are oral options, and the clinician reviews on a 12-week cycle rather than monthly. The clinician decides which program fits your intake.

Which formulation will I get?

Your clinician chooses from seven, based on your intake. Options include oral and injectable forms with NAD+, B12, L-carnitine, inositol, magnesium, L-theanine, methionine or choline.

What does the price include?

The medication, supplies, unlimited clinician consults, shipping, and patient support around the clock. There is no separate consultation or physician fee.

What if I am not prescribed?

Nothing is ordered and your program payment is refunded in full.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time for any reason from your patient portal. Any month whose medication has not been ordered is refunded.
