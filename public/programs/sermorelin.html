What sermorelin is

Sermorelin is a peptide analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone, the signal the body uses to prompt its own growth hormone release. It is given as a small injection under the skin at a dose your clinician sets.

The compounded version is prepared by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy to your clinician's prescription. Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

Sermorelin questions, answered

Is sermorelin the same as growth hormone?

No. Sermorelin is an analog of the releasing hormone, not growth hormone itself. Whether it is appropriate for you is the clinician's decision.

Do I need labs?

Not as a rule. Your clinician will tell you if labs are recommended for you.

What does the price include?

The medication, injection supplies, clinician reviews, shipping, and patient support around the clock. There is no separate consultation or physician fee.

What if I am not prescribed?

Nothing is ordered and your program payment is refunded in full.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time for any reason from your patient portal. Any month whose medication has not been ordered is refunded.
