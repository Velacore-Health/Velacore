The clinicians who review your intake

Every intake is reviewed by a physician or nurse practitioner who holds an active license in your state. The clinician is independent of Velacore, and the prescribing decision is theirs alone.

Who reviews your intake

Your intake is reviewed by a licensed physician or nurse practitioner. The clinicians are medical doctors, doctors of osteopathic medicine and nurse practitioners.

They are engaged through our telehealth services partner, not by Velacore. Your clinician may vary based on your state and availability.

Every program on this site is prescription only. Prescription required.

Licensed in your state

The clinicians are licensed across all 50 states. Your intake goes to a clinician who holds an active license in the state where you live.

Velacore telehealth services are available in all 50 US states and the District of Columbia. Every prescription requires review by a clinician licensed for your state.

Credentialed before they review an intake

Our telehealth services partner credentials each clinician before that clinician reviews a single intake. The process covers these checks.

  • A primary source background check.
  • A review of education and training.
  • A review of work history.
  • A review of board certification.
  • A review of state board standing and state licenses.
  • Verification of the National Provider Identifier.
  • A review of liability coverage and malpractice history.
  • A review of federal and state sanction and exclusion lists.
  • A review of the National Practitioner Data Bank.
  • Signed attestations from the clinician.

Malpractice coverage is in place for the clinicians.

Independent of Velacore

Clinicians are independent providers. They are not employed by Velacore Health, and Velacore does not direct their decisions.

Every prescribing decision is made at the clinician's own clinical discretion. Velacore coordinates access and education. It does not prescribe, and it has no say in whether a clinician prescribes.

Hours

Clinician hours are 7am to 7pm Central Time and do not include holidays.

Patient support is separate from clinician hours. It is available 24 hours a day, every day.

Telehealth is not for emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Video visits

Most reviews happen without a live appointment. The clinician reads your intake and decides.

A live video visit is required for the first prescription in these states.

  • Arkansas
  • District of Columbia
  • Delaware
  • Kansas
  • Mississippi
  • New Mexico
  • Rhode Island
  • West Virginia

Everywhere else a video visit is optional. You can request one at any time at no extra cost.

What a clinician can decide

  • Whether to prescribe at all. A prescription is not guaranteed.
  • Which program, if any, is appropriate for you.
  • The form and the dose, within the options of the program.
  • Whether to hold or change a dose at each review.
  • Whether labs are recommended. For the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs, baseline labs are ordered at a national laboratory at no cost to you.
  • For the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs, whether to prescribe an optional nausea medication that you pick up at a local pharmacy.

If the clinician does not prescribe, nothing is ordered and your program payment is refunded in full. The order of events is set out on the how it works page.

Common questions

Are the clinicians employed by Velacore?

No. They are independent providers engaged through our telehealth services partner. Velacore does not direct their decisions.

Can I ask for a video visit even if my state does not require one?

Yes. You can request a video visit at any time at no extra cost.

Can the clinician decide on a different program from the one I picked?

Yes. You choose the program you want to start with, and the clinician decides which program, if any, is appropriate. If the clinician does not prescribe, your program payment is refunded in full.

When are clinicians available?

Clinician hours are 7am to 7pm Central Time and do not include holidays. Patient support is available 24 hours a day, every day.
