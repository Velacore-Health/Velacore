Pricing

Each program has one price, shown before you start. There is no separate consultation or physician fee. Prescription required.

What one price includes

The program price covers the parts of the service that would otherwise be billed one by one.

  • The medication, if the clinician prescribes it.
  • The clinician's review of your intake and of each intake update after that.
  • A video visit on request, at no extra cost.
  • Patient support 24 hours a day, every day.
  • For the Semaglutide and Tirzepatide programs, baseline labs at a national laboratory.

There is no separate consultation fee and no separate physician fee. Each program page lists exactly what its price includes.

Two ways to pay

Each program offers two plan types.

  • Month to month. You are charged each month. You can cancel at any time.
  • Prepaid plan. You are charged once, up front. Medication still ships every four weeks. The 12-week plan is priced below, and 24 and 52 week plans are also offered.

You choose the plan when you start. The clinician's review is the same on both.

What is not included or not accepted

  • Insurance. Programs are paid out of pocket and are not billed to insurance.
  • American Express. It is not accepted at this time.
  • Returns. Medications are non-returnable once dispensed, as required by federal and state pharmacy law.

Payment methods

Accepted payment methods are Visa, Mastercard, FSA and HSA cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Billing is handled by our telehealth services partner. Its name is the one you will see on your card statement. Velacore does not hold your card details.

Refunds and cancellation

If the clinician does not prescribe, nothing is ordered and your program payment is refunded in full.

You can cancel at any time, for any reason, from your patient portal or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future charges. A month whose medication has already been ordered will still ship and is not refunded.

You can request a refund at any time for any reason. The refund covers every billing period for which medication has not yet been ordered. On a 12-week plan, the shipments not yet ordered are refunded on the same basis.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. The full terms are in the refund policy.

Common questions

Is there a consultation fee?

No. There is no separate consultation fee and no separate physician fee. The clinician's review is part of the program price.

Can I pay with an FSA or HSA card?

Yes. FSA and HSA cards are accepted, along with Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Can I bill a program to my insurance?

No. Programs are paid out of pocket and are not billed to insurance.

What happens to a 12-week plan if I cancel?

Cancellation stops future charges, and the shipments not yet ordered are refunded. A month whose medication has already been ordered will still ship and is not refunded. The full terms are in the refund policy.

Whose name will be on my card statement?

Our telehealth services partner's. It handles billing and issues refunds.
