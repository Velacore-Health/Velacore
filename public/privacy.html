Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what the Velacore Health website collects, how it is used and who receives it. Velacore Health is operated by First Venture Group LLC ("Velacore," "we," "us"). This policy covers this website and emails you send us. It does not cover the secure intake, patient portal, billing or medical records run by our telehealth services partner, the clinicians and the pharmacy. Those are covered by their own privacy notices, which you see when you start a visit.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: if you email us, we receive your name, your email address and whatever you write. This website has no forms, no accounts and no checkout.
  • Automatic information: device, browser, IP address, and usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Ad tags in the page address: if you arrive from an ad, the address may carry tags that start with utm_. If you continue to the secure intake, those tags travel with the link so our partner can tell which ad you came from. Our analytics provider also records them on the pages where it runs.

We do not collect health information, intake answers, identity documents or card details on this website. When you start a visit, you leave this website for our telehealth services partner's secure site, and their privacy notice applies there.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • To answer your emails.
  • To pass a request on to our telehealth services partner when you ask us to, such as a billing or refund request.
  • To comply with legal requirements.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide the service, including with:

  • Our telehealth services partner, only when you ask us to pass on a request, such as a billing or refund question.
  • Service providers that support this website, such as our web host, analytics provider, font provider and email provider.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your health information & HIPAA

Licensed clinicians and pharmacies that provide care to you are generally covered entities under HIPAA and maintain their own privacy practices.

Data security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting hello@velacorehealth.com. Certain records may be retained as required by law.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets cookies in your browser and receives your IP address, device details and the pages you view. Our own code sets no cookies. The site's typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without them.

Children

Our services are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.
