About Velacore Health

Velacore Health is a telehealth coordination platform. We connect adults in the United States with independent licensed clinicians and licensed 503A compounding pharmacies.

Who operates Velacore

Velacore Health is operated by First Venture Group LLC.

How it works

Three parties, three jobs.

  1. Velacore coordinates. We run this website, explain the programs and their prices, and point you to the secure intake.
  2. An independent clinician decides. A physician or nurse practitioner licensed in your state reviews your intake and decides whether a prescription is appropriate. That decision is theirs alone.
  3. A 503A pharmacy fills. If you are prescribed, a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy prepares your medication and ships it to you.

The intake, the clinician review, billing and patient support are run by our telehealth services partner on its own secure pages. Each step is set out on the how it works page.

What we offer access to

Six programs: Tirzepatide, Semaglutide, Microdosing GLP-1, NAD+, Glutathione and Sermorelin. Every one is prescription only. Prescription required.

Compounded medications are not FDA approved and are not reviewed by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not practice medicine or give medical advice.
  • We do not prescribe, and we have no say in whether a clinician prescribes.
  • We do not make, compound or ship medication.
  • We do not collect your health information on this website.
  • We do not hold your card details. Our telehealth services partner bills you.
  • We do not bill insurance.
  • We do not promise that you will be prescribed anything.

What you can expect from us

  • Prices shown before you start.
  • A plain explanation of each step.
  • Cancel any time, for any reason.
  • A full refund of your program payment if the clinician does not prescribe.
  • Straight answers by email at hello@velacorehealth.com.

Where we serve

All 50 US states and the District of Columbia. Every prescription requires review by a clinician licensed for your state.
