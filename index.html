FAQ

Questions, answered

What are peptides?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the building blocks of proteins — that can play signaling roles in the body. In a clinical setting, specific peptide therapies may be prescribed to support goals such as metabolic health, recovery, or vitality. Whether a peptide is appropriate for you is a decision made with a licensed clinician.

How does the telehealth process work?

You complete a secure online health assessment, a U.S.-licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — prescribes a personalized protocol. Your therapy is compounded at a licensed pharmacy and shipped to your door, with ongoing support from your care team.

Is peptide therapy right for me?

That's exactly what the free eligibility check helps determine. Eligibility depends on your goals, medical history, and current medications. Your clinician will recommend a path — or let you know if therapy isn't appropriate.

Where are the medications made?

Prescribed therapies are compounded in licensed U.S. pharmacies that adhere to applicable quality, sterility, and testing standards.

Which states do you serve?

We're expanding availability. During the eligibility check you'll confirm your state so we can match you with a clinician licensed where you live.

Do you take insurance?

Membership and compounded therapies are typically paid out of pocket. We aim to keep pricing simple and transparent — you'll see costs before you commit.
