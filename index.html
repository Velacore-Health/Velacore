How it works

From evaluation to fulfillment

Velacore coordinates each step. An independent licensed physician makes every medical decision.

  1. 00

    Free wellness evaluation

    Start with a free Velacore wellness evaluation to assess your goals, lifestyle, and baseline health markers. Determines the right program path before any medical review.

  2. 01

    Choose a program path

    Based on your evaluation, we help you identify the structured wellness program path that fits your goals.

  3. 02

    Independent physician review

    Your information is shared with an independent, licensed physician who reviews it and makes the medical decision — approve, adjust, or decline.

  4. 03

    Pharmacy fulfillment

    If a program is approved, it's fulfilled by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy and shipped to you with instructions.

  5. 04

    Ongoing coordination

    Message the Velacore care team for coordination and support throughout your program.

  6. 05

    Not approved? Full refund

    If the independent reviewing physician does not approve your program, you're refunded in full — including the physician review fee — within 15 days.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Velacore Health?

Velacore Health is a telehealth coordination platform. We provide structured wellness resources and coordinate access to independent licensed physicians and 503A compounding pharmacies. We do not provide, guarantee, or facilitate any medical services, prescriptions, or treatments — all medical decisions are made solely by the independent provider.

How does the process work?

You start with a free wellness evaluation, choose a program path, and — if you proceed — your information is reviewed by an independent, licensed physician who makes the medical decision. Approved programs are fulfilled by a licensed 503A compounding pharmacy and shipped to you.

Does Velacore prescribe or provide medical care?

No. Velacore coordinates access and logistics. Independent, licensed physicians make all clinical decisions, and licensed 503A pharmacies handle fulfillment.

What is your refund policy?

If the independent reviewing physician does not approve your program, you will be refunded in full — including your program payment and the physician review fee — within 15 days. Medications are non-returnable once dispensed.

Which states do you serve?

Velacore Health telehealth programs are currently available to residents of select US states. Contact our care team to confirm availability in your state.

How do I get started?

Begin with the free wellness evaluation. It helps determine the right program path before any medical review — with no obligation.
