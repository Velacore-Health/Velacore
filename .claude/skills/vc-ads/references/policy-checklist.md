# Policy checklist for Velacore ads

Run every ad, landing page and audience through this list. One fail blocks the item. Policy pages change; before any submission, reread the live policy at the links at the bottom and update this file with the date checked.

## Gate: certification and account standing

- [ ] LegitScript certification issued in Velacore Health's name, with the certificate number on file. Meta and Google both require it for online pharmacy and prescription telehealth advertisers in the US. Not issued yet as of Nov 6 2026.
- [ ] Platform permission granted: Meta's online pharmacy and prescription drug advertising permission, Google's healthcare and medicines certification. Both are applications made after LegitScript and both are Dourado's to submit.
- [ ] The advertising domain is the certified domain, velacorehealth.com, and the landing page is on it. No ads point at the Shopify store or a third-party page.
- [ ] Every ad account, page and pixel is in Velacore Health's name, owned by First Venture Group LLC, not mixed with any other business.

## Claims and copy

- [ ] Only products on the public site are named. Nothing restricted, nothing "coming soon".
- [ ] No efficacy or outcome claims: no weight amounts, no percentages, no timeframes, no "works", "proven", "clinically shown", "guaranteed", "safe".
- [ ] No before and after images, no body transformation framing, no implied negative self-image. Meta's personal health and appearance rules reject these.
- [ ] No brand-name drug comparisons. No "same as", "generic", "alternative to" or "like" a brand. No brand names at all.
- [ ] No "FDA approved" and nothing that implies it. Compounded medications are not FDA approved and the ad or its landing page must say so.
- [ ] "Prescription required" and "an independent licensed clinician decides" appear on every landing page, and in the ad where space allows.
- [ ] No coaching, no "program coach", no "we'll help you lose", no lifestyle promises.
- [ ] Price shown is the real price with the real conditions. "$149 first month, then $229 a month, cancel any time" in full. No hidden price jump, no "from $99" that needs a prepay to be true.
- [ ] Testimonials and reviews: none until there are real, consented, unpaid ones with the typical result disclaimer. Never written for someone.
- [ ] Nothing targets, mentions or implies a medical condition the viewer has. The ad talks about the service, not the person.

## Targeting and placement

- [ ] Age 18 and up on every campaign. GLP-1 programs start at 18 and OpenLoop's age limit is 18 to 74.
- [ ] United States only, and only states OpenLoop serves, which is all 50 plus DC.
- [ ] No interest or behavior targeting that names a health condition, weight, or medication. Broad targeting plus creative does the work.
- [ ] No retargeting audiences built from health pages or intake visits beyond what the platform allows for the category. Default: no retargeting until the policy page is rechecked.
- [ ] No lookalikes built from patient lists. Patient data never leaves OpenLoop.

## Landing page

- [ ] Loads on velacorehealth.com, over HTTPS, in under three seconds on a phone.
- [ ] Carries the compounded-medication disclaimer, prescription required, the refund terms in one sentence, and links to privacy, terms, telehealth consent and refund policy.
- [ ] The Start visit button goes to the OpenLoop hosted intake for that product, with UTMs intact.
- [ ] No pop-ups that block the disclaimers. No countdown timers or fake scarcity.
- [ ] Passes `vc-compliance-check` and `vc-site-audit`.
